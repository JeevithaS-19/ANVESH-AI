ANVESH AI – Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 19, 2026

1. Introduction

ANVESH AI is an AI-driven voice and messaging assistant designed to help users explore livelihood, career, training and skill-development opportunities.

2. Information We Collect

Depending on how the service is used, ANVESH AI may process information provided by users, including educational qualifications, skills, work experience, interests, career goals and location.

When users communicate through WhatsApp, the service may also process their WhatsApp phone number and messages sent to the ANVESH AI service.

3. How We Use Information

Information is used to create a beneficiary profile, identify skill gaps, match users with suitable occupations and training opportunities, and provide personalized recommendations.

4. Data Sharing

ANVESH AI does not intentionally sell users' personal information. Information may be processed through service providers and APIs required to provide the application's functionality.

5. Data Security

Reasonable technical measures are used to protect information processed by the application. However, no internet-based system can guarantee absolute security.

6. Data Retention

Information is retained only for as long as reasonably necessary to provide, maintain, test or improve the service, subject to applicable requirements.

7. User Rights

Users may request information about their personal data or request deletion of information associated with their use of the service, subject to applicable requirements.

8. WhatsApp

When ANVESH AI is accessed through WhatsApp, messages are processed to provide the requested conversational and recommendation services. WhatsApp's own terms and privacy policies also apply to WhatsApp users.

9. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when the application or its data practices change. The latest version will be published on this page.

10. Contact

For privacy-related questions, please contact the ANVESH AI development team through the contact information associated with the application.
